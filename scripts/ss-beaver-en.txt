You've reached Prospect Point. Look toward the water below. Near here, in 1888, a ship called the S.S. Beaver ran aground. The Beaver holds a remarkable place in history. Launched in 1835, it was the first steamship on the Pacific Northwest coast. It was owned by the Hudson's Bay Company. Imagine it: the very first steam-powered ship working this coastline. More than fifty years after it was launched, its journey ended right here, near Prospect Point. Fifty-three years from launch to wreck. When you look out at the boats crossing the water today, remember that the first steamship on this coast finished its travels within sight of where you're standing. Take a breath, enjoy the view, and when you're ready, ride on. Our final stop is a giant of a different kind: a tree that was already centuries old long before the Beaver was launched.
