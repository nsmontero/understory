Up ahead is Lumbermen's Arch. Here's a small puzzle: the arch you can see is not the original. The first Lumbermen's Arch was built in 1912 as a tribute to the logging industry. A year later, in 1913, it was moved here, to the park. It stood for more than three decades, until it was demolished in 1947. For a few years, there was no arch at all. Then, in 1952, the arch you see today was installed. So this one spot has carried the same idea through two different structures: a salute to the people and the industry of logging. As you pass, take a look at it, and imagine the first arch standing here more than a century ago. Two arches, one tribute. From here, the seawall carries you north toward Prospect Point, where a famous ship met its end.
