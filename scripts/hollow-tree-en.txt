This is the Hollow Tree. It's a western red cedar, and it is estimated to be between 700 and 800 years old. Let that number settle for a moment. When this tree began to grow, nothing you've heard about on this ride existed yet. No seawall. No arch. No steamship. No poem. Seven or eight centuries is a very long time for any living thing to stand in one place. As its name tells you, the tree is hollow, and you can see that for yourself. Take your time here. Walk around it, look up, and notice the sheer size of it. Think back over the ride: a poem from 1910, a wall that took more than fifty years to build, two arches, a steamship from 1835. This tree was already old for all of it. That's the end of our tour. Thank you for riding with us through Stanley Park. We hope you'll come back and discover more of its stories.
